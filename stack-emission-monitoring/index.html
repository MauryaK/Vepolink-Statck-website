How it works

From analyser to plant head,
under five seconds.

A resilient acquisition path preserves every sample and exposes the same governed record to operators, managers and regulators.

  1. 01Stack analyser

    Extractive or in-situ measurement of gases, particulate matter and process references.

  2. 02Industrial DAS

    Multi-protocol acquisition, timestamping, local buffering and field diagnostics.

  3. 03Vepolink cloud

    Normalisation, reference-O2 correction, validation and secure time-series storage.

  4. 04Live operations

    Stack, plant and group views for operators, supervisors and compliance teams.

  5. 05Portal & action

    OCEMS transmission, pre-limit alerts, audit trails and scheduled reports.
