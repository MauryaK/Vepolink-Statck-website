Overview

Fixed Gas
Detector

The Fixed Gas Detector continuously monitors industrial environments for combustible and toxic gas leaks. Using electrochemical and catalytic sensing technologies, it delivers accurate gas concentration measurements with configurable alarm thresholds. Designed for hazardous locations, the detector supports multiple output interfaces, integrates with PLC, DCS, and SCADA systems, and provides reliable protection for personnel and industrial assets.

06 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the Fixed Gas Detector?

It’s a continuous fixed gas detection system for industrial safety that monitors toxic and flammable gases 24/7.

Which gases can the Fixed Gas Detector monitor?

It detects a wide range of gases including CH₄, CO, H₂S, SO₂, NH₃, NOx, Cl₂, O₃, HCN, and O₂ levels with accurate gas concentration detection.

Does this fixed gas detector support alarms and alerts?

Yes. It provides alarm alerts (audible/visual) and control panel integration when gas concentrations exceed safe limits.

How is the detector connected and powered?

The system operates on 24 V DC with standard 4-20 mA and RS-485 outputs to interface with control systems and PLC/SCADA.

Is the Fixed Gas Detector suitable for industrial safety compliance?

Yes — it’s ideal for industrial leak detection, workplace safety, and hazardous area monitoring to protect personnel and assets.
