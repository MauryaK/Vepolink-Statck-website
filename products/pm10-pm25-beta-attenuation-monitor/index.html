01 · Overview

Continuous particulate mass, measured not modelled.

A continuous, real-time beta attenuation monitor for ambient PM10 and PM2.5. Particulate is collected on a glass fibre filter tape and its mass determined directly from the attenuation of beta radiation - the nationally and internationally recognised technique for regulatory particulate measurement.

An in-line sampling geometry measures beta attenuation across the filter media while sampling continues, so readings accumulate through the whole hour rather than in short windows. The system runs unattended, 24 hours a day.

02 · Measurement principle

How beta attenuation weighs air.

Beta rays lose energy in proportion to the mass they pass through. Collect particulate on a filter tape, measure the beta signal before and after, and the difference is a direct measurement of collected mass.

Beta attenuation

Mass by radiation loss

A low-activity carbon-14 source emits beta particles through the collected dust to a detector. The count rate falls as mass accumulates.

Size selection

Inlet head & cyclone

A PM10 size-selective inlet is paired with a sharp-cut cyclone for PM2.5. Active flow control keeps the size fraction stable.

Sample conditioning

In-situ thermal control

The beta measurement is held slightly above ambient to prevent excess moisture and semi-volatile loss without a membrane dryer.

Data path

Reporting & integration

Hourly and averaged values are logged internally and pushed to the cloud or a regulatory server over Ethernet, RS-485 MODBUS, 4G and analog.

03 · Measurement cycle

One hour, four steps.

Sampling and counting happen together, so the instrument spends the hour measuring rather than switching between collection and reading.

Step 01

Fresh tape advance

A clean section of glass fibre tape is positioned in the sampling head.

Step 02

Continuous sampling

Ambient air is drawn through the tape at a regulated 16.7 L/min.

Step 03

Attenuation compare

The loaded count rate is compared against the reference to determine collected mass.

Step 04

Report & repeat

The value is logged, transmitted and the cycle restarts.

06 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the working principle?

Beta ray attenuation. Particulate collected on glass fibre filter tape attenuates beta radiation in proportion to its mass.

Can it measure PM2.5 and PM10?

Yes. A PM10 size-selective inlet and sharp-cut cyclone provide the required fractions.

How long does one measurement take?

One hour is standard, with configurable intervals where site conditions require it.

Does the beta source need special handling?

The low-activity carbon-14 source is sealed within the instrument and supplied in the exempt category.

Does the shelter need air conditioning?

No. In-situ thermal control handles moisture without a membrane dryer or air conditioner.

How quickly can it be deployed?

No component weighs above 18 kg and typical setup from arrival to running data is under ten minutes.
