Overview

In-Situ Tunable
Laser Analyzer

In-Situ Tunable Laser Analyzer is a highly precise laser gas analyzer based on Tunable Diode Laser Absorption Spectroscopy (TDLAS). It uses a tunable semiconductor laser to scan the specific absorption lines of the measured gas and determine gas concentration from the second harmonic signal. Its one-side mounting installation simplifies installation compared with cross-stack systems and eliminates the need for complex optical path adjustment.

Applications

Where it works.

The tunable diode laser emits specific wavelengths through the gas path. The receiver processes the resulting signal and determines gas concentration from the second harmonic signal and gas absorption characteristics.

06 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the In-Situ TDLAS Gas Analyzer?

It is a high-precision industrial gas analyzer using Tunable Diode Laser Absorption Spectroscopy (TDLAS) for direct, in-situ gas concentration measurement.

How does the TDLAS gas analyzer measure gases?

It uses a tunable laser to scan specific absorption lines and calculates gas concentration from the second harmonic signal without requiring sample extraction.

What are the key benefits of this analyzer?

It provides non-contact optical measurement, low drift, fast response of less than 1 second, and low maintenance requirements.

Where is the In-Situ TDLAS Analyzer used?

It is used for continuous emissions monitoring, industrial process gas analysis, and environmental compliance applications in power, petrochemical, and manufacturing plants.

Is installation complicated for this TDLAS gas analyzer?

No. The in-situ installation does not require complex optical path adjustment and is designed for reliable operation in industrial environments.
