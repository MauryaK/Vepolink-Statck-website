Overview

Portable Multi
Gas Detector

The Multi Gas Detector is a compact handheld safety instrument designed to detect multiple gases simultaneously. It provides continuous monitoring of combustible gases, oxygen, carbon monoxide (CO), hydrogen sulfide (H₂S), and other toxic gases. Ideal for hazardous industrial environments, mining operations, confined spaces, oil & gas facilities, and underground utilities, the detector offers reliable measurements with high sensitivity and repeatability.

06 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Which gases can this detector measure?

It can monitor combustible gases, oxygen (O₂), carbon monoxide (CO), hydrogen sulfide (H₂S), and other toxic gases depending on the sensor configuration.

Is the detector suitable for confined space monitoring?

Yes. It is specifically designed for confined space entry and hazardous industrial environments.

Does it support data logging?

Yes. The detector stores up to 1200 records and allows USB data transfer for analysis.

What sampling method does it use?

It supports natural diffusion sampling and optional auto suction sampling.
